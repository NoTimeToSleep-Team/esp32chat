# Legal templates (draft)

These are operator templates, not legal advice. Have a qualified lawyer review
them for the jurisdiction and the camp's child-protection policy. Keep signed
copies separate from operational data and limit access to director/DPO.

## 1. Parent/guardian consent for camp processing

> I, ______________________, parent/guardian of ______________________
> (child, date of birth __________), consent to the processing of the child's
> name, unit, room, badge identifier, dietary/medical notes needed for care,
> attendance, pickup authorisations and operational safety events for the
> duration of the camp session and the stated retention period.
>
> I understand that the camp uses an offline local system, that the child does
> not have free-form private chat with adults, and that operational logs may be
> reviewed for safety and audit.
>
> Retention period: ______ days after the session ends.
>
> Signature: ______________________  Date: __________

## 2. Photo/media consent matrix

> For the child named above, please choose one or more:
>
> - [ ] `internal_chat` — photos may be shared with camp staff in the internal
>   chat for care/safety.
> - [ ] `parent_digest` — photos may appear in the digest visible to me.
> - [ ] `promo` — photos may be used in public/promo materials (separate
>   withdrawal right applies).
> - [ ] `none` — do not use the child's photo/media.
>
> A missing choice means **no consent**. I may withdraw consent in writing;
> withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing.
>
> Signature: ______________________  Date: __________

>
> Authorised adult: ______________________

> Document checked: ______________________
>
> Restrictions (e.g. only this adult, both parents, ID required):
> ____________________________________________________________
>
> Valid from: __________  Valid to: __________
>
> Parent/guardian signature: ______________________  Date: __________
> Camp director/security: ______________________  Date: __________

> The QR code is an operational token. It must not be posted publicly. A

## 4. Emergency medical consent (short form)

> I authorise camp staff to seek emergency medical care for my child when I
> cannot be reached. I will provide separate medical/allergy information to the
> camp medic. This does not replace emergency services.
>
> Signature: ______________________  Date: __________

## 5. Retention and deletion request

> I request deletion of my child's camp data after the retention period, except
> records the camp is legally required to keep. I understand that aggregate,
> non-identifying statistics may be retained.
>
> Signature: ______________________  Date: __________

## 6. Operator notes

- Store signed forms in a locked location or an encrypted DMS, not in the camp
  SQLite database.
- Do not put passport numbers, medical diagnoses or custody documents in free
  text fields.
- For a minor's data, follow local child-protection and data-protection law.
- Document the lawful basis, retention period and who can access the data.
