One man thought he was very clever. He said to himself: "If I put water in instead of wine, nobody will see it, because there will be so much wine that the water will disappear in it".

On the night of the feast, all the people came to the square with their cups. They opened the tap, but only water came out. Everyone in the village had had the same thought
